Common Questions

Does SpendStory require an account?

No. SpendStory is designed to work without an account.

Where is my expense data stored?

The current version stores your expense data locally on your device.

Does SpendStory sell my data?

No. SpendStory does not sell your personal information.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete expenses inside the app. You may also remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.
