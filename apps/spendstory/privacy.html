SpendStory is designed as a local-first personal expense tracker and financial insight app.

SpendStory được thiết kế như một ứng dụng quản lý chi tiêu local-first và phân tích tài chính cá nhân.

1. Data stored by the app

SpendStory may store expense records, notes, categories, spending types, jars, reminders, onboarding preferences, export/import JSON backups, and timeline markers locally on your device.

2. Local-first storage

The current version of SpendStory does not require user accounts and does not operate a backend syncing service for your spending history.

3. Data collection

SpendStory does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal spending records with the developer or third parties.

4. Notifications

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device when you enable reminders.

5. Import, export, and deletion

You may export JSON backups, import compatible backups, and delete local data from inside the app.

6. Third-party services

The current version does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs. Exchange-rate requests may use a third-party exchange-rate provider without including your personal spending history.

7. Financial disclaimer

SpendStory does not provide investment, accounting, tax, or legal advice.

8. Security

SpendStory relies on iOS sandboxing and Apple device-level protections.

9. Children’s privacy

SpendStory is not intended to knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated if SpendStory features or data practices change.
