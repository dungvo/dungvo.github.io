SpendStory Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

SpendStory is designed to help you record expenses and understand your spending insights while respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

SpendStory does not require an account. The app is designed to store your expense data locally on your device. This may include amounts, categories, notes, dates, spending types, jars, cadence information, and other details you enter in the app.

How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used to provide app features such as expense tracking, summaries, dashboards, reports, insights, financial health views, and spending stories.

Data Sharing

SpendStory does not sell your personal information. SpendStory does not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

Analytics and Tracking

The current version of SpendStory does not use third-party advertising tracking. If analytics, cloud sync, or other online services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what data is used and why.

Local Storage

Expense data is stored on your device. Deleting the app may delete locally stored data unless your device or a future app feature provides backup or sync.

Children’s Privacy

SpendStory is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
