- Nguồn: Johannes Wendsche và Andrea Lohmann-Haislah, *A Meta-Analysis on Antecedents and Outcomes of Detachment from Work*, Frontiers in Psychology 7:2072, DOI `10.3389/fpsyg.2016.02072`.
