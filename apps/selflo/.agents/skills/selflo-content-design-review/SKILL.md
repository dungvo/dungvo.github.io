# Selflo Content Design and Review

Read `apps/selflo/AGENTS.md`, content workflow, the work package, source/lifecycle guides, and shared contracts when applicable. Use the content review template.

Define audience, purpose, outline/components, claim–source mapping, uncertainty, forbidden claims, provenance/rights, channel target, and compatible/app-impacting/synchronized contract class. Review through product, editorial Vietnamese, source, rights, knowledge, safety, schema/component, app capability, and Release lenses.

Every finding needs evidence and disposition. Do not invent citations, infer rights, turn literary copy into scientific evidence, or copy shared semantics into app-facing docs. Stop for owner on sensitive voice/claim, unresolved rights/source, shared contract/cutover, or Release acceptance.
