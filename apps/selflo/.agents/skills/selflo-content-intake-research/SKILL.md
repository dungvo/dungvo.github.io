# Selflo Content Intake and Research

Read `apps/selflo/AGENTS.md`, work-in-progress ledger, content workflow, relevant lifecycle and shared contract hub. Locate an existing work package before creating one. Use `apps/selflo/docs/templates/content-assessment-template.md`.

Separate need, audience/channel, observed sources, assumptions, evidence against, provenance, rights, claim risk, competing concepts, and app/contract impact. Do not treat Candidate, generated Authoring/Release/API, or owner preference as canonical evidence/source.

Return `go`, `needs-clarification`, `park`, or `kill`. Go permits research/design or Authoring preparation only; it does not authorize rights confirmation, contract activation, Release, `--push`, or app changes. Update the WIP ledger and end with exactly one next step.
