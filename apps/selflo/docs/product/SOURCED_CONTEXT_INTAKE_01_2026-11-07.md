## Source and rights controls
Each entry includes `author`, `source_work`, `source_url`, `source_section`, `rights`, `rights_evidence_url`, `teaser_author`, `full_text_ingested=false`, `review_status`. 'Public domain in USA' is **not** an assertion of universal worldwide public domain. Check the local law and translations. Do not assume a modern Vietnamese translation is free to republish.
