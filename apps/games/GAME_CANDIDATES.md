## Product boundary

Games Hub is a standalone collection at `/apps/games/`, independent from Selflo, English, and SpendStory. Existing Selflo games are **reference prototypes**; do not move, remove, overwrite, or change them without an explicit decision. Avoid copying Selflo-specific reflection, energy, quotes, or stories as mandatory game mechanics. First review existing gameplay and visual quality, then select ONE game for a focused vertical slice. Do not build all candidates simultaneously.

## Selected game and other shortlisted candidates

**Selected:** Hành Trình Đom Đóm (Firefly Adventure). **Alternates retained:** Ánh Sáng / Light Quest and Mystery Room / Mystery Adventure. This is a product direction decision, not a claim that the game has been migrated or built in Games Hub.

## Three shortlisted candidates

### 1. Ánh Sáng / Light Quest — logic and replayability
- **Existing source:** [Visual Game Lab](../../selflo/studio/developer/visual-games/) — select **Ánh sáng**. Source: `apps/selflo/studio/developer/visual-games/visual.js`, `lightsStart()`.
- **Verified mechanics:** touching a tile toggles itself and its orthogonal neighbors; objective is to light all tiles. Existing sizes: easy 3×3, medium 4×4, hard 5×5. Levels generated by applying moves to the solved state; thus solvable. Current generator does not guarantee consistent difficulty or optimal move counts.
- **Development direction:** standalone Light Quest; curated/procedurally verified levels, 3-star goals tied to proven minimum moves, chapter map, animated light propagation, sound, accessible controls, hints with real solver, special tile mechanics introduced gradually, daily challenges only if fun.
- **Prototype milestone:** 15–20 polished levels with tested solutions and gradual difficulty, satisfying touch/lighting feedback, mobile-first.
- **Main risk:** repetitive mechanics or unfair difficulty if levels are random without solver validation.

### 2. Hành Trình Đom Đóm — atmosphere and adventure
- **Existing source:** [Selflo Adventure](../../selflo/studio/developer/adventure/) with `adventure.js`.
- **Verified prototype:** 3 worlds: Rừng Sớm (exploration), Hồ Trăng (key), Đền Sao (traps). Canvas world, movement controls, collect light fragments and open the gate.
- **Development direction:** independent firefly adventure with stronger art direction, lighting, motion, ambient sound, varied terrain and hazards, characters, collectibles, checkpoints, distinct world identities and meaningful progression. Keep gameplay central; reflective text optional.
- **Prototype milestone:** one exceptionally polished world with several short stages and a memorable end-of-world reveal.
- **Main risk:** visual production cost and movement/control feel on mobile.

### 3. Mystery Room / Mystery Adventure — curiosity and discovery
- **Existing sources:** [Game Lab](../../selflo/studio/developer/game-lab/) (Căn phòng bí ẩn) and [Hồ sơ Ánh Trăng](../../selflo/studio/developer/detective/) (3 investigation cases).
- **Verified detective prototype:** three cases: Chiếc đồng hồ mất tích, Bức thư bị đánh tráo, Bí mật trong thư viện; clickable scene hotspots, evidence and on-scene clue tooltip/dialogue.
- **Development direction:** visually rich interactive rooms, clues displayed *inside the scene*, inventory and combining items, linked multi-step puzzles, recurring characters, environmental storytelling and chapter-level mysteries. Avoid a question-and-answer-only experience.
- **Prototype milestone:** one polished room with 10–15 meaningful interactive hotspots, several interconnected puzzles, and an ending that invites a next chapter.
- **Main risk:** high level-authoring cost; one-off puzzles may limit replayability.

## 2026-11-08 — Firefly RPG direction (design proposal, not yet implemented)

User requests a much deeper Firefly game, taking inspiration from the *experience* of Fire Emblem and TearRing Saga: character leveling, HP, weapons, equipment, collectible items, compelling narrative, branching choices, replaying to discover substantially different routes. Do not copy copyrighted characters, story, maps, art, or music.

**Design goal:** A standalone adventure/tactical RPG, not merely collecting light dots in a maze. The existing `/apps/games/firefly/` is a playable **exploration prototype**, NOT this RPG.

**Proposed pillars:**
1. Character growth: HP, XP/levels, equipment, weapons, skills; growth through combat and exploration.
2. Tactical combat with meaningful positioning, enemy behavior and risk/reward, preferably turn-based.
3. Items with gameplay and narrative consequences (keys, relics, tools, healing, equipment).
4. Branching story state: decisions affect allies, accessible routes, missions, rewards, world state and endings. Avoid fake choices that immediately converge without consequences.
5. Replayability: multiple discoverable routes, hidden events, alternate recruits and optional discoveries; keep an event/choice registry to ensure consistency.
6. Atmosphere: preserve Firefly's luminous fantasy art direction, compelling characters, smooth mobile interaction.

**Example narrative concept (unapproved):** Chapter 1, a village losing its light. Player may rescue villagers, help a wounded gatekeeper or rush to a temple. Each path yields different ally/item/knowledge and changes later events. This is a design illustration, not canon.

**Scope gate:** Before a large RPG build, create a small but complete vertical slice: one chapter, 2–3 consequential routes, 1 tactical combat encounter, XP/HP/weapon, a few usable items, and at least one replay-only discovery. Validate whether it is genuinely fun on mobile before adding dozens of chapters.

**Status:** User's requested direction recorded. Specific combat rules, narrative canon and production scope still require design/review. No claim that these systems have been implemented.
